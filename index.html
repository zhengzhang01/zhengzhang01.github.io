Zheng Zhang

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Hengshuang Zhao.

My research focuses on 3D computer vision, video geometry, and self-supervised visual representation learning. I am interested in learning the structure of the 3D world from images and videos.

I have been a research intern at Ant Research, Alibaba DAMO Academy, and Shanghai AI Laboratory. I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2023.

I am looking for research positions in industry starting in 2027.
